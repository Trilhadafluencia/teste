Understanding the Verb "To Be"

Forma

The verb "to be" has different forms depending on the subject and tense. Here’s a brief overview:

  • Present: am, is, are
  • Past: was, were
  • Future: will be

Uso

The verb "to be" is used to express existence, identity, and qualities. Here are some examples:

  • I am a teacher.
  • She is happy.
  • They are students.

Exercises

Please complete the following sentences:

  1. I ___ (be) a doctor.
  2. They ___ (be) friends.
  3. We ___ (be) ready.
